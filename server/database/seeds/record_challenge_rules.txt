GENERAL
Set the first record
Choose an open challenge and submit your result and evidence. Every challenge starts open. The first attempt Ampex approves establishes the official record. Later attempts must exceed the verified count or hold time, or finish faster in speed events. Matching a result leaves the existing record in place.
Show the setup
Identify your challenge on video and show the equipment, starting positions, and required measurements before the attempt. Use one continuous, unedited recording from setup through final count or finish. A second original angle, timing footage, or official race result may supplement the main video.
Time the attempt
Use a visible timer or a clear, audible start signal and a fixed camera frame rate so reviewers can check the time from the footage. For timed counts, begin on the start signal and score only actions completed by the time limit. Any required stability check takes place after the time limit. For fastest-time tasks, start on the signal and stop at the finish point described in the rules. For holds and continuous juggling, use the stated start and stop conditions.
Submit the score
Report the valid count or elapsed time, the challenge number, the attempt date, and the original video. Report elapsed time to the precision supported by your timing evidence. If the record uses a final stack, pile, or target, film it and count the qualifying items before moving them.
Keep the attempt verifiable
Keep the participant, relevant movements, and scoring area visible. A helper may time or film the attempt but may only assist with the challenge if its rules allow it. Repetitions that miss the stated requirements earn no credit. If the evidence cannot establish the setup, timing, or score, the attempt cannot be verified.
SECTION Fitness Challenges
01  Most push-ups in 1 minute
Count valid reps in 60 seconds.
Start on the floor in a high plank with arms straight, hands under or just outside the shoulders, and only hands and toes supporting you.
Lower until the upper arms are at least parallel with the floor, then return to straight arms. Keep the body in a straight line from shoulders to ankles. Count at the top. You may pause in the high plank. The attempt ends if the knees or torso rest on the floor.

03  Most sit-ups in 1 minute

Lie on your back with knees bent, feet flat and unanchored, and arms crossed over your chest. Start with both shoulder blades on the floor.
Rise until both elbows touch your thighs, then lower until both shoulder blades touch the floor again. Count on the return to the floor. Keep your feet down and arms crossed. You may pause between reps.

Keep the torso and knees still and the free hand off your body and the weight. You may rest at the bottom with the dumbbell in that hand. Setting it down ends the attempt.
Film from an angle that shows the working elbow, shoulder, hips, knees, and dumbbell throughout.

13  Longest time holding a push-up position
Score the duration of one continuous high-plank hold.
Start timing when both arms are straight and only hands and toes are supporting you. Keep hands under or just outside the shoulders and maintain a straight line from shoulders to ankles.
Stop at the first loss of the straight body line, bend of either supporting elbow, lift of a hand or foot, or contact by any other body part. Film from the side with your entire body visible.
14  Most bodyweight squats in 1 minute
Count valid reps in 60 seconds.
Start standing with hips and knees straight. Squat until the hip crease reaches knee height or lower, then return to standing with hips and knees straight to score one rep. Keep both heels down. You may pause upright.
Use no weights or support. Film from the side at a height that clearly shows the hips and knees.
15  Most alternating reverse lunges in 2 minutes
Count valid reps in 120 seconds. Each completed lunge counts as one rep.
Start standing. Step one foot back, lower until the rear knee lightly touches the floor or a thin mat, then return both feet to the starting position with hips and knees straight.
Alternate sides on counted reps. If a rep fails, repeat that side for the next score. Keep the front foot planted and your hands off your legs and any support. Film both feet and the rear knee.
16  Longest forearm plank hold
Score the duration of one continuous forearm plank.
Start timing when your body is supported by both forearms and toes, with elbows under shoulders and a straight line from shoulders to ankles.
Stop at the first loss of the straight body line, lift of a forearm or foot, or contact by any other body part. Film from the side with your entire body visible.
17  Most jump-rope skips in 1 minute
Count successful skips in 60 seconds.
Start with the rope still and both feet on the floor. Swing a standard handheld jump rope forward. One jump with one complete rope pass under the feet scores one skip.
Restart after a miss while the timer continues. Double-unders, backward passes, and passes without a jump score zero. Keep the rope and feet visible.
18  Most strict pull-ups without dropping from the bar
Count completed reps in one continuous hang. Rest is allowed while hanging.
Start with both hands gripping the bar palms forward and arms straight, with feet clear of the ground. Raise your chin above the top of the bar, then return to straight arms to score one rep.
No kicking, kipping, leg drive, or assistance. Stop when either hand releases the bar, your body touches the ground or another support, or you perform a rep using momentum.

SECTION Funny Talent Challenges
23  Most successful basketball free throws in 1 minute
Count successful shots in 60 seconds.
Use a 10-foot hoop, a standard size 7 basketball, and a free-throw line 15 feet from the face of the backboard. Show the distances or court markings. A helper may return balls. Start holding one ball behind the line.
Remain behind the line until the shot touches the rim or backboard or passes through the hoop. A basket scores when the ball passes completely through from above before time expires. Film the line, shooter, and hoop together.
24  Most soccer ball juggles without the ball touching the ground
Count consecutive legal touches in one run.
Use a standard size 5 soccer ball. Begin by releasing it from your own hands or lifting it with a foot. The first legal contact starts the count. Feet, legs, and torso may touch the ball. Hands, arms, and head are excluded.
Score one per distinct legal touch. The run ends when the ball touches the ground, a prohibited body part, an object, or another person. Keep the full body and ball path in frame.
25  Longest time juggling tennis balls
Score the duration of one continuous three-ball juggle.
Use three standard tennis balls. Juggle alone in a three-ball cascade, alternating hands and throwing each ball to the opposite hand. Start timing when the third ball is first released into the pattern.
Stop when a ball touches the floor, another surface, or another person, or when the pattern stops. Holding any ball for more than one second ends the attempt at the start of that hold. Keep all three balls and both hands in frame.
26  Most socks put on one foot in 30 seconds
Count socks fully on the same foot after 30 seconds.
Use matching adult crew socks, with each sock laid out separately. Start seated, with the chosen foot bare and your hands clear of the socks. Use both hands if desired.
Put socks on one at a time. To count, a sock must cover the toes and heel, with its opening above the heel at the buzzer. No pre-layered socks or help from another person.
27  Fastest time to put on 10 T-shirts
Score the shortest time to put on all 10 T-shirts.
Use 10 ordinary short-sleeved T-shirts that fit over your base layer and are laid out individually. Start with hands off the shirts. Put on the shirts one at a time over a base layer that does not count.
Each shirt must have your head through the neck, both arms through the corresponding sleeves, and its hem pulled below the waist. Stop when the tenth shirt is fully on. Show all 10 layers on camera after the attempt.
28  Most clothespins clipped onto a T-shirt in 30 seconds
Count clothespins still attached at 30 seconds.
Wear a regular T-shirt. Put identical spring clothespins loose on a table and start with hands clear. Clip each pin directly to the shirt fabric.
Pins on skin, other clothing, or another pin do not count. Pins must stay attached without support at the buzzer. Film the attached pins closely before removing them.
29  Most gift bows stuck onto a sweater in 30 seconds
Count gift bows still attached at 30 seconds.
Wear an ordinary sweater. Lay out matching peel-and-stick gift bows with their backing intact. Start with hands off the bows. Peel and stick one at a time directly to the sweater.
Bows stuck to other bows, skin, or other clothing do not count. No added tape or adhesive. Show the attached bows before removing them.
30  Most paper balls thrown into a laundry basket in 1 minute from 6 feet away
Count successful throws in 60 seconds.
Use paper balls made from one standard printer sheet each and an empty laundry basket with an opening no wider than 18 inches in any direction. Mark a line 6 feet from the nearest edge of the opening and show the measurement. Lay out the pre-rolled paper balls before starting.
Throw one item at a time from behind the line. A score counts when the item enters and stays inside the container through the end of the attempt. Throws made while stepping on or over the line score zero. Use each item once and leave the container in place.
Film the throwing line, the opening, and the final contents.

32  Most ping-pong balls bounced into cups in 1 minute
Count successful bounces in 60 seconds.
Use 12 identical 16-ounce plastic cups, openings up, in two rows of six on a hard table. Arrange each row with rims just touching. Mark the release line 2 feet from the nearest cup rim and keep the throwing hand behind it.
Bounce one standard ping-pong ball at a time off the tabletop before it enters a cup. Each ball that remains in a cup at the buzzer counts, including multiple balls in the same cup. Do not move or empty cups after starting. Film the bounce and targets.
33  Most cotton balls moved between bowls with a spoon in 30 seconds
Count cotton balls in the receiving bowl at 30 seconds.
Place two bowls on a table with their nearest rims 12 inches apart. Start with standard cotton balls in one bowl and the other bowl empty. Start holding one ordinary tablespoon clear of the balls.
Transfer one ball at a time by spoon. Keep the bowls still. Do not touch balls with your hand or retrieve dropped balls. Film both bowls and count the receiving bowl contents after time.
34  Fastest time to stack 10 plastic cups into a pyramid and take it down
Score the lowest elapsed time for one complete stack and takedown.
Start with 10 identical plastic cups nested in one stack, rims down, and hands off the cups. Build a freestanding pyramid with the rims down and four cups on the bottom, then three, two, and one.
Release the completed pyramid before taking it down. Time stops when all cups are back in one nested stack, rims down, with hands released. You may fix dropped or misplaced cups while time runs.
35  Most toilet paper rolls stacked into a freestanding tower in 30 seconds
Score the height in rolls of the tallest qualifying tower.
Start with identical full toilet paper rolls set out separately and hands off. Build one vertical column, with each roll flat on the one below and the bottom roll directly on the table.
Release the tower by 30 seconds. Only one freestanding column that remains upright for three seconds after the buzzer counts. No rods, adhesive, or side supports.

Build one vertical stack without outside support or adhesive. Release it by the buzzer. It must stand for three seconds after time, with only the bottom coin touching the table.

Toss the bottle so it rotates end over end at least once and lands upright on its base. It must settle before you touch it again. A successful landing must settle by the buzzer. You may pick up misses while time continues.

Launch one airplane at a time from behind the line. A plane scores if first floor contact is inside the hoop and it comes to rest entirely inside before the buzzer. Use each plane once and film the line and hoop together.
39  Most cushions carried across a room in one trip without dropping any
Count cushions carried over a 10-foot course in one trip.
Mark start and finish lines 10 feet apart. Use matching square cushions approximately 18 inches per side. Load them yourself behind the start line. Start upright behind the line holding the full load.
Carry the whole load until you and all cushions clear the finish line. No straps, bags, carts, or outside help. Dropping any cushion between the lines invalidates the trip. Show the distance, the crossing, and the final count.
40  Fastest time to fold and stack 10 bath towels while wearing oven mitts
Score the fastest time to fold and stack all 10 towels.
Lay out 10 matching bath towels approximately 27 by 52 inches, flat and unfolded. Wear one full oven mitt on each hand. Start with your mitts off the towels.
Fold each towel individually in half lengthwise and then in half crosswise. Place the towels in one freestanding stack. Time stops when the tenth folded towel is on the stack and both mitts are clear. Keep both mitts on and show the final stack.
